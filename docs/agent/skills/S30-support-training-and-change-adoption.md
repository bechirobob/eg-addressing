# S30 — Support, Training, and Change Adoption

## Invoke when

- changing helpdesk, support, onboarding, training, SOPs, or release communication;
- preparing field, municipal, ministry, agency, or national rollout;
- measuring adoption, support burden, workflow understanding, or operational change.

## Required inputs

- affected roles, institutions, regions, languages, devices, and support hours;
- current/future workflow and release scope;
- identity/scope/readiness boundary;
- training fixture/environment rules;
- support, accessibility, privacy, and incident standards.

## Procedure

1. Identify roles, institutions, regions, languages, accessibility/connectivity needs, and support ownership.
2. Define current versus future workflow, authority/responsibility changes, migration, user impact, support impact, and rollout wave.
3. Create role-specific training using safe fixtures and the exact application version.
4. Distinguish training/simulation from official operation/publication.
5. Produce accessible Spanish and English quick starts, task guides, supervisor/admin guides, troubleshooting, privacy/security, and escalation paths.
6. Define helpdesk categories, severity, SLA, evidence, privacy, escalation, and closure.
7. Give support staff safe diagnostics without excess authority.
8. Define release notes, known limitations, maintenance/downtime communication, rollback communication, and contact ownership.
9. Run training/pilot exercises and observe completion, errors, excessive steps, misunderstanding, support demand, and workarounds.
10. Classify feedback as defect, usability, training, policy, data quality, infrastructure, or architecture.
11. Measure active use, task completion, error/recapture, support cases, training completion, institutional/region coverage, and satisfaction without invasive analytics.
12. Feed lessons into backlog, runbooks, training, standards, and work orders.

## Outputs

- role/institution impact assessment;
- bilingual accessible training pack;
- helpdesk taxonomy/escalation matrix;
- release/change communication;
- exercise and adoption results;
- feedback-to-backlog traceability.

## Stop or RFI conditions

Stop when:

- users would be onboarded before identity/scope/authority readiness;
- training uses real sensitive data unnecessarily;
- support requires privileges beyond remit;
- defective workflow is being disguised as a training issue;
- official operation lacks named operational ownership.

## Evidence gate

Before review:

- training matches the exact release and safe environment;
- role-language-accessibility coverage is complete;
- helpdesk/escalation and diagnostic access are tested;
- pilot/training observations and adoption measures are recorded;
- support, communication, and known limitations have owners.

## Anti-patterns

- Shipping features with no operational documentation.
- One manual for every role.
- Training screenshots from another version.
- Production credentials/data in training.
- Measuring adoption only by logins.
- Closing support cases without root-cause classification.
